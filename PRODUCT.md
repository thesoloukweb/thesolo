# The Solo Kitchen & Bar

register: brand

## Product purpose

Marketing website for a Mediterranean restaurant, cocktail bar and rooftop terrace in Bethnal Green, East London (thesolo.co.uk). The site exists to get a table booked. Everything else (menus, events, private hire, brunch, reviews) supports that one action.

## Users

- Locals and East London visitors deciding where to eat or drink tonight or this weekend, mostly on a phone, often in the evening.
- Groups planning bottomless brunch, birthdays and parties (party menus, drinks packages, private hire brochure).
- People who already know the place and want hours, the address, the phone number or a menu PDF fast.

## Brand and tone

- Warm, confident, unfussy British hospitality. Sunshine, cocktails, a good time; never stiff, never corporate.
- Real: photos and film shot in the venue, reviews pulled from Google, facts identical to the Google Business Profile (phone 020 8014 2656, opening 10:00 daily).
- Visual identity from the client's Figma: deep green (#102B2A), copper (#C49A85), Crimson Text for display, Josefin Sans for text.

## Anti-references

- Generic "restaurant template" sites: stock photos, centred everything, three identical cards per row.
- SaaS look: glass cards, gradient text, neon on black, Linear or Apple clones.
- Heavy dark washes that make the venue look gloomy. The place is bright and green in daylight and warm at night; the site must show that.

## Strategic principles

1. Reservation is the primary conversion; it must be reachable from every screen without hunting.
2. Performance is part of the design: mobile LCP must stay a small image, media loads after the page is interactive.
3. Nothing invented: hours, address, events and reviews come from the client or Google, never from copy filler.
4. Accessible by default: WCAG contrast on brand copper (dark text on copper), pause control for motion, reduced-motion respected.
