### Medium (300–400 chars — for TripAdvisor, OpenTable bio)

We serve authentic Turkish, Greek, Italian and Middle Eastern cuisine, with all-day breakfast and brunch, signature cocktails, and a stunning rooftop terrace. Live Sunday Jazz Sessions every Sunday from 12 PM. Private dining and event spaces available. 20% Blue Light Card discount.

### Long (full description — for Facebook About, Foursquare, blogs)
> The Solo Kitchen & Bar is a premier Mediterranean restaurant in the heart of East London, located at Museum Gardens, Cambridge Heath Road, E2 9PA — moments from Bethnal Green station. We bring together the vibrant flavours of Turkey, Greece, Italy and the Middle East with fresh ingredients, passionate cooking, and a warm, welcoming atmosphere.
>
> Our menu features all-day Breakfast & Brunch (served until 3 PM), an extensive À La Carte menu, signature cocktails, and party-friendly sharing platters. Our rooftop terrace offers panoramic views of London and is home to our beloved Sunday Jazz Sessions — live jazz every Sunday from 12 PM to 3 PM.
>
> We host private events and celebrations from intimate dinners to gatherings of 200+ guests. Family-friendly, casual smart dress code, wheelchair accessible. We proudly offer a 20% discount for Blue Light Card holders.
>
> Open 7 days a week from 8:30 AM. Reservations: thesolo.co.uk/reservation or 020 8014 2656.
